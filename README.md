# EasyRap

**Turn one or two photos into a short rap music video.**

[Open EasyRap](https://easyrapvideo.com/) · [Pricing](https://easyrapvideo.com/pricing) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

EasyRap is a browser-based AI tool for creating personalized rap music videos from photos and an optional idea or short lyrics.

## What you can create

- **Birthday rap:** personalize a birthday shout-out.
- **Friendly roast:** turn a harmless habit or an inside joke into a rap clip.
- **Pet rap:** create a rap video from a pet photo.
- **Rap duet:** create a short two-lead rap video.
- **Your own idea:** choose a style and describe the scene you want.

## How it works

1. Choose an idea and add one or two reference photos. A photo of exactly two people is also supported; Pet Rap uses a pet photo.
2. Pick a style and optional scene, then add a personal detail or short lyrics if you want.
3. Choose 5, 10 or 15 seconds, a supported aspect ratio and quality setting.
4. Review the available settings and credit cost before confirming generation.
5. Once generation succeeds, play or download the MP4 from My Videos.

Available styles include Street Rap, Trap, Old School, Funny Rap and Anime Rap. Options and availability may change; the website is the source of truth.

## Pricing

Video generation uses paid credits, available through credit packs and subscription plans. There is currently **no free rendered trial**. Preparing photos and settings does not spend video credits; each confirmed new generation is charged separately.

See [current pricing](https://easyrapvideo.com/pricing) and review the credit total in the creator before confirming. Credit cost depends on duration and quality.

## Important limitations

- Outputs are AI-generated; results may vary.
- Exact lyrics and precise lip sync are not guaranteed.
- Reference-audio or music-file uploads, Suno/Udio links and voice cloning are not supported.
- Use images you have permission to use. Any real people shown must be consenting adults.
- Do not use EasyRap to impersonate people or claim an endorsement that does not exist.
